# 47-windows-board-archive

Archive and restore individual roots and physical groups on the Windows grouped board. Port the [compared root archive](37-browser-root-archive-restore.md) and [group recovery](38-browser-group-archive-restore.md) as one board Archive workflow. Use both GPUI skills and reuse the existing Rust lifecycle operations, board access decision and bounded archive queries. Keep the direct-board contract and existing flat reads intact.

An Owner can archive a root by ID after confirmation, or archive a group by its heading menu. Group archive leaves child records untouched and offers transient Undo. Opening Archive after a new visit must show archived groups and effective item states from the server, including the reason an individual root cannot yet be restored. Undo and group restore call the same operation. Members and Viewers with access can inspect recovery; only a current Owner can mutate it. Respect revision checks, accepted order, cancellation, session loss and read-only recovery after uncertain results. Never replay on refresh.
